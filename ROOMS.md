# The six rooms

The book is read like a day: you open the cover in the bedroom and end the evening on the balcony.
Every room is a corner diorama — two paper walls and a folding floor — plus a few pop-up cut-outs
that stand up only once the room is fully open. Rooms start as blue-ink line art and wash with colour
as they come to the front.

Wall A is the left wall as you look into the room, wall B is the right wall.

| # | Room | Mood | Wash palette |
|---|------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Bedroom | slow mornings, late-night thinking | lavender walls, oak floor, rose accents |
| 2 | Bathroom | resets, mirror talks | sea-glass mint, white tile, coral |
| 3 | Kitchen | nourishment, home, people over | butter yellow, terracotta, sage |
| 4 | Gym | body, discipline, anger worked out | brick red, cream, charcoal, electric blue |
| 5 | Lounge | play, games, doing nothing | teal, peach, mustard |
| 6 | Balcony | evenings, the big picture, gratitude | dusk blue to violet, warm deck wood, fairy-light yellow |

## 1. Bedroom (with table) — redesigned

Blue ink on white paper, no colour: every piece is a paper cut-out with a white margin and a soft
shadow (after Dakshi's reference sketch).

- **Left wall** — big window with clouds, stars near the corner, two taped photos near the front
  edge.
- **Right wall** — hanging plant on three cords near the corner, stars, a big cork board with
  pinned notes, and the desk drawn flat on the wall (drawers, lamp, laptop, mug, pencil cup, book).
- **Floor** — boards, scalloped rug, slippers, soft shadows under the furniture.
- **Pop-ups** (drawn isometrically so they read as solid furniture from the camera)
  - curtains on a rod hanging just off the left wall, either side of the window
  - a simple bed (frame, mattress, quilt with a folded edge, pillow)
  - in front: chair at the desk, woven waste bin
- **Entries hung here** — dreams, sleep, late-night thoughts, what you're studying.

## 2. Bathroom — redesigned

Same paper cut-out style as the bedroom, after Dakshi's reference sketch; ink-only or soft colour.

- **Left wall** — tiled shower recess with a shower head and falling water and a shelf with two
  bottles; a towel on a hook near the front edge, sparkles, and a hanging plant near the corner.
- **Right wall** — framed picture, two floating shelves (folded towels, reed diffuser, a jar and a
  little trailing plant), the toilet drawn flat on the wall, toilet-paper holder, arched mirror with three sticky notes and a wall
  lamp above it, sparkles.
- **Floor** — square tiles, a small blue mat by the shower, a big mat in the middle, soft shadows.
- **Pop-ups** — shower curtain on a rod just off the left wall, pulled to the inner end; vanity with two doors, basin, faucet, soap pump and toothbrush cup; a
  woven bin with a towel over the edge.
- **Colour version** — sea-glass walls and shower tiles, pale blue curtain and vanity, coral mats
  and towel, wooden shelves.
- **Entries hung here** — resets, self-talk, routines, things you wash off.

## 3. Kitchen — redesigned (after Dakshi's reference)

Same paper cut-out style; ink-only or soft colour.

- **Left wall** — window with clouds and curtains on a rod, two floating shelves near the corner
  (a leafy plant and books, two jars), tiled backsplash, and the counter drawn flat on the wall
  (three doors, a tea towel, sink rim, gooseneck tap, soap, kettle); a trailing plant and two
  taped notes (heart, checklist) near the front edge.
- **Right wall** — framed picture, range hood, upper cabinet with a plant on top, tiled
  backsplash, and the counter drawn flat (door, oven, drawers, kettle, utensil jar, pot,
  cutting board, fruit bowl).
- **Floor** — boards and a scalloped rug, soft shadows.
- **Pop-ups**
  - a tall fridge with notes and a plant on top
  - round table with flowers in a vase, a fruit bowl and a mug; spindle-back chair and a stool
- **Colour version** — butter-yellow walls, sage cabinets, pale blue fridge, coral curtains, kettle
  and rug, wooden floor and furniture.
- **Entries hung here** — food, family, cooking experiments, people you fed or who fed you.

## 4. Gym / boxing / body

- **Wall A** — long mirror, dumbbell rack, a scribbled "one more" poster.
- **Wall B** — window, skipping rope on a hook, wall clock, gloves hanging.
- **Floor** — rubber mat squares, a rolled yoga mat.
- **Pop-ups** — hanging punching bag, kettlebell, water bottle.
- **Entries hung here** — workouts, body, energy, anger and how you worked through it.

## 5. Lounge (chill sofa / games)

- **Wall A** — TV on a low console with a game console and controllers, shelf of games.
- **Wall B** — framed posters, bookshelf, string lights, a clock.
- **Floor** — shaggy rug, snack bowl.
- **Pop-ups** — sofa with cushions, bean bag.
- **Entries hung here** — fun, games, shows, friends, doing nothing on purpose.

## 6. Balcony

- **Wall A** — railing with hanging plants, dusk sky with moon and stars above.
- **Wall B** — railing, city skyline with lit windows, fairy-light string.
- **Floor** — wooden deck planks.
- **Pop-ups** — two folding chairs, small round table with chai, tall potted plant.
- **Entries hung here** — evenings, gratitude, the big picture, what you want next.

## Later

- Frames on the walls become your real entries; the number of frames grows as you write.
- Clicking a wall opens that room's board (dropped for now, see the plan).
- Pop-ups could gently animate on hover (curtain sway, punching bag swing, steam from the chai).
